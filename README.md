# Guo-He Li (李国禾) — Academic Monograph Homepage

Scholarly dual-column homepage featuring a sticky profile dossier, a serene astronomical observatory banner, and dedicated illustrated research modules for **Early Universe** and **EMRIs & Gravitational Waves**.

## Assets

- `assets/hero-sky.jpg` — Serene starlit horizon & distant observatory banner artwork.
- `assets/cosmos-early.jpg` — Cosmic web & primordial structure artwork inside the Early Universe research module.
- `assets/cosmos-emri.jpg` — Black hole & dark-matter halo artwork paired with the interactive EMRI orbit & strain simulation inside the EMRI & GW research module.
- `assets/portrait.jpg` — Place your rectangular 4:5 portrait photo here (automatically displayed on load; local preview supported via the hover button).

## Local Preview & Deployment

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```
Open `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.
